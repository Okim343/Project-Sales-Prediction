# Protection-window replenishment backtest

## Prespecified design and decision rule

The protection windows **7, 14, and 28 days** and service levels **0.5, 0.8,
0.9, and 0.95** are placeholders until the business supplies lead time, review
period, and service target. Each window starts the day after its cutoff. Window
means are sums of daily conditional means. Daily quantiles are never summed.

The primary quantile method is a negative binomial distribution with variance
`mu_W + phi * mu_W²`. `phi` is estimated by a nonnegative method of moments
from observed-sales residuals at three weekly inner origins, each ending its
longest window by the outer cutoff. Eligibility and volume tier are recalculated
at each inner origin. A tier with fewer than 150 series-origin pairs pools both
tiers. Near-zero `phi` uses Poisson; zero `mu_W` yields zero quantiles.

The robustness method estimates empirical quantiles of `actual_W / mu_W` on the
same inner origins when `mu_W >= 1`. It falls back to the negative binomial below
that floor. This method can absorb mean bias into its P50 and does not determine
the selected mean model. The LightGBM-only challenger directly fits a quantile
model for each window and service level, with weekly training origins from the
last 365 days, window targets ending by the cutoff, origin-anchored sales and
price features, lag-364 window sales, and known holiday counts. Its predicted
quantiles are sorted and rounded up to whole units.

**Decision fixed before reviewing outer-fold scores:** for each window, consider
`lgbm_direct`, `lgbm_blend_50_50`, and `lgbm_recursive` under `nb_tier`. For each
source and target (real observed, synthetic observed, synthetic true demand),
average scaled pinball over the four service levels, then take the equal-weight
mean of those three scores. Require real observed coverage at 0.9 within ±0.03
in **both** tiers. Among models that pass, choose the lowest composite pinball;
within 1% relative, choose lower excess units at 0.9 on real observed sales. If
none passes, report the model with the smallest worst-tier absolute coverage
gap as closest, plus the pinball leader, and withhold a definitive selection.
Compare the outcome with `weekday_mean_4w` under the same `nb_tier` method.
Assess `lgbm_quantile` separately against the selected mean plus `nb_tier`.

The single-period simulation sets the order-up-to level to each integer window
quantile. It reports fill rate, stockout-window rate, excess units relative to
actual, and mean days of cover held. Synthetic results use both observed sales
and planted true demand. Real results can only use observed sales, which may be
censored by stockouts.

## Run and main finding

This is the prespecified four-cutoff comparison on the common top-100 set. The real source had fewer than 100 eligible series at its first three cutoffs. The exact reproducible outputs are in the local ignored `bld/backtest/` folders listed below.

The full requested command completed successfully in about **10 minutes 45
seconds**, below the 25-minute target. The replenishment phases took 3.0 minutes
on real data and 4.7 minutes on synthetic data. Every eligible common-set series
received a window forecast for every selected mean model; there were **zero
missing series**. The production XGBoost model was excluded from the
replenishment step because `--replenishment-include-current` was not set.

The 1% pinball tie-breaker applies only among coverage-qualified models. No settings were changed after seeing outer-fold results.

At alpha 0.9, the provisional closest LightGBM means have real observed fill
rates of **0.93–0.94** and excess of **0.58–0.69 times** actual demand across the
three windows. Their top-tier coverage is only **0.81–0.84**. The 7-day weekday
baseline achieves both-tier coverage within three points of 0.9 and a lower
three-target composite pinball than any LightGBM mean, but with much higher
excess (0.92 times actual units versus about 0.58). Thus it is the safer
7-day coverage benchmark, not a cost-free replacement.

## Estimation counts and limitations

- `real`: 90 of 144 model/window/tier/cutoff cells pooled tiers; inner samples ranged from 150 to 276 pairs.
- `synthetic`: 0 of 144 model/window/tier/cutoff cells pooled tiers; inner samples ranged from 161 to 705 pairs.

These windows and service levels are placeholders. The real actuals are stockout-censored sales; synthetic true demand is the closer replenishment target. The simulation is a single-period newsvendor calculation, not a multi-period inventory policy with on-hand stock, open orders, lead-time variability, or lost-sales feedback. Next, obtain lead-time and review-period distributions, inventory/stockout records, and a service target; validate the chosen distribution on new cutoffs and then simulate the full stock policy before deployment.
